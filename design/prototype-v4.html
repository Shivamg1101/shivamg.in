    How the systems work

    Two builds in more depth — the design decisions and what they changed.

    Grounding a support agent so it refuses to guess

    Inbound queries hit a retrieval layer over the brochure corpus, chunked into a Qdrant vector store with Gemini embeddings. The model answers only from retrieved passages and emits a structured object carrying both the reply and an escalation flag; refunds, complaints and account-specific issues route to a human rather than receiving an invented answer. The constraint is the product — a confidently wrong reply about a refund costs more than no reply at all.

    Replacing a manual daily review with targeted escalation

    Every evening the workflow joins counsellor rosters, walk-in logs and live MCube telephony data, computes role-specific call targets — different for counsellors, team leads and telecounsellors, adjusted down per walk-in handled — and emails only the agents below target, copying their manager. What used to be a manager reading a spreadsheet each night is now an exception report that arrives on its own.
