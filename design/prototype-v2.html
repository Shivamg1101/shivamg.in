I turn manual operations into systems that run themselves.

I joined SkillCircle in February 2025 as an IT Executive, managing hosting for 1,200+ domains — cPanel, DNS, SSL, Linux. I started automating the repetitive parts of that job, the automations became core operations, and in January 2026 the company created the AI & Automation Engineer role for it.

That path shapes how I build. I write read-only dry runs and diagnostic probes before anything touches production data, and keep write operations disabled until I’ve verified the inputs. Automation that runs unattended against real student records has to be boring and correct.
